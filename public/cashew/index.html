About us

Brand story

Brief introduction

Born in the heart of Binh Phuoc – Vietnam's renowned cashew-growing region – Hat Ngoc Xanh was founded with a simple yet meaningful mission: to bring clean, nutritious, and high-quality products from our homeland to every family. At Hat Ngoc Xanh, we believe that good nutrition is the foundation of a better life. That is why we carefully select our ingredients, prioritize food safety, and uphold strict quality standards throughout every stage of production. Every product we offer reflects our commitment to purity, honesty, and excellence.
